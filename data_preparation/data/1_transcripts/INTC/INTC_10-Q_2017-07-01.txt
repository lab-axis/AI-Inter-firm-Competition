&#8226;

&#8226;

In Q2 2017 &#32;we achieved revenue of $14.8 billion , an increase of $1.2 billion &#32; or 9% &#32;from Q2 2016 . After adjusting for the divested Intel Security Group (ISecG), revenue grew 14% from a year ago. Compared to Q2 2016 , our topline growth of $1.2 billion &#32;was primarily driven by strong performance of our Client Computing Group (CCG) and our data-centric businesses* also grew 16% year-on-year. Non-Volatile Memory Solutions Group (NSG) set record revenue of $874 million , up &#32; 58% &#32;from Q2 2016 . Earnings per share were $0.58 , up &#32; 31 cents &#32;on a year-on-year basis, primarily driven by the restructuring charges in Q2 2016 and the result of strong topline growth, partially offset by the impacts from the ISecG divestiture. 

&#8226; 

CCG had revenue of $8.2 billion , up &#32; 12% &#32;with platform volumes up &#32; 3% &#32;and platform average selling prices up &#32; 8% &#32;compared to Q2 2016 . The PC market is slightly ahead of our expectations and we continue to see the worldwide supply chain operate at healthy levels. 

&#8226; 

Data Center Group (DCG) had revenue of $4.4 billion , up &#32; 9% &#32;with platform volumes up &#32; 7% &#32;and platform average selling prices up &#32; 1% &#32;compared to Q2 2016 . 

&#8226; 

Gross margin of 61.6% &#32;was up 2.7 points &#32;compared to Q2 2016 . 

&#8226; 

Research and development (R&#38;D) plus marketing, general, and administrative (MG&#38;A) spending for the quarter was $5.1 billion , flat &#32;from a year ago. R&#38;D and MG&#38;A were 34.7% &#32;of revenue, down approximately 3 &#32;points from Q2 2016. To increase efficiency as a company, we expect to continue reducing our spending as a percent of revenue and reach a spending target of approximately 30% of revenue by 2020. 

&#8226; 

Operating income for Q2 2017 &#32;was $3.8 billion , up &#32; 190% &#32;on a year-on-year basis. The tax rate for the quarter was 38.6% , up 18.2% &#32;compared to Q2 2016 &#32;due to the tax expense related to the divestiture of ISecG. Net income for Q2 2017 &#32;was $2.8 billion , up &#32; 111% &#32;from Q2 2016 . 

&#8226; 

Our business continues to generate healthy cash flow with $4.7 billion &#32;of cash from operations in Q2 2017 . During Q2 2017 , we purchased $2.8 billion &#32;in capital assets, paid $1.3 billion &#32;in dividends, and used $1.3 billion &#32;to repurchase 36 million &#32;shares of stock. 

We introduced the new Intel&#174; Core&#8482; X-series processor family in Q2 2017 . Intel&#8217;s most scalable, accessible and powerful desktop platform to date, it includes the new Intel&#174; Core&#8482; i9 processor brand and the Intel&#174; Core&#8482; i9 Extreme Edition processor &#8211; the first consumer desktop CPU with 18 cores and 36 threads design. 

In early Q3 2017, we launched the Intel&#174; Xeon&#174; Scalable Processors, formerly known as Skylake. The new product represents significant performance improvement over our prior generation products across a wide range of real-world workloads in data centers. We have already delivered more than 500 thousand units to over 30 customers through our early ship program. 

During Q1 2017, we entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Mobileye N.V. (Mobileye). We expect the transaction to close during Q3 2017, pending satisfaction of all closing conditions. We intend to fund the acquisition with cash and proceeds from the sale of assets within our investment portfolio held by our non-U.S. subsidiaries. &#32; 

* Data-centric businesses include DCG, Internet of Things Group (IOTG), NSG, Programmable Solutions Group (PSG), and all other.

Net Revenue 

Our net revenue in Q2 2017 &#32; increased &#32;by $1.2 billion , or 9% , compared to Q2 2016 . The increase in revenue was driven by higher platform unit sales, up &#32; 5% , and higher platform average selling prices, up &#32; 6% , primarily from our notebook and DCG platforms. Additionally, revenue increased from our higher NSG revenue and CCG non-platform revenue. The increase in revenue was offset by $537 million &#32;from the Q2 2017 &#32;divestiture of ISecG. 

Our net revenue for the first six months &#32;of 2017 &#32; increased &#32;by $2.3 billion , or 9% , compared to the first six months &#32;of 2016 , which reflected an extra workweek. The higher revenue was driven by higher platform average selling prices, up &#32; 7% , and platform unit sales, primarily from our notebook and DCG platforms. Additionally, revenue increased from higher NSG and CCG non-platform revenue. The increase in revenue was offset by the Q2 2017 &#32;divestiture of ISecG and lower desktop platform unit sales.

We derived most of our overall gross margin dollars from the sale of platforms in the CCG and DCG operating segments. &#32;
