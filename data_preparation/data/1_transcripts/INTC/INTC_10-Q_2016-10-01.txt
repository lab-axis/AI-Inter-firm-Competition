In Q3 2016 &#32;we achieved record revenue of $15.8 billion , up 9% &#32;from Q3 2015 , with strong growth in Client Computing Group (CCG) and record revenue from both Data Center Group (DCG) and Internet of Things Group (IOTG). The revenue results included Programmable Solutions Group (PSG) revenue from the newly acquired Altera Corporation (Altera). Q3 2016 &#32;revenue was above our Business Outlook, which was revised upward in September 2016 from our original expectations as a result of inventory replenishment and better than expected PC demand. 

Gross margin of 63.3% , including approximately 1.5 points of amortization of acquisition-related intangibles, was 3.3 points above the midpoint of our original expectation of 60.0% and was up 0.3 point s from Q3 2015 . Compared to Q3 2015 , the gross margin percentage was positively impacted by lower platform unit costs and higher platform average selling prices, which were partially offset by higher factory startup costs on 10nm, lower Non-Volatile Memory Solutions Group (NSG) profits, and amortization of acquisition-related intangibles. Spending on R&#38;D and MG&#38;A for the quarter was $5.1 billion , down $77 million &#32;from Q2 2016 , driven by deeper cuts associated with our restructuring program partially offset by an increase in profit dependent spending. There were $372 million &#32;in restructuring and other charges for the quarter. Operating income for Q3 2016 &#32;was $4.5 billion , up &#32; 6% &#32;on a year-on-year basis. The tax rate for the quarter was 21.8% . Net income for Q3 2016 &#32;was $3.4 billion , up &#32; 9% &#32;on a year-on-year basis. Earnings per share were $0.69 , up &#32; 5 cents &#32;on a year-on-year basis. 

In September 2016, to further accelerate our transformation and focus our business on core strategic areas, we announced the planned divestiture of our Intel Security business. The planned divestiture along with the 2016 Restructuring Program, which is on-track to achieve the run rate savings and employment reductions announced earlier this year, resulted in additional restructuring and other charges in Q3 2016 . For additional information about the restructuring programs as well as the divestiture of Intel Security, see "Restructuring and Other Charges" in the MD&#38;A and "Note 8: Acquisitions and Divestitures" respectively. 

Compared to Q3 2015 , CCG revenue was up &#32; 5% &#32;and operating income increased &#32; 37% . DCG revenue was up &#32; 10% &#32;and operating income was down &#32; 1% &#32;as we increased investments and continued to ramp the first 14nm DCG product. IOTG revenue was up &#32; 19% &#32;and operating income was up &#32; 27% . NSG revenue was down &#32; 1% &#32;and operating loss was at $134 million . PSG recognized revenue of $425 million , up 6 percent when compared to Altera&#8217;s results from a year ago, and operating income was at $78 million . Intel Security Group (ISecG) revenue was up &#32; 6% &#32;and operating income was up &#32; 19% . 

We ended Q3 2016 &#32;with $17.8 billion &#32;of cash and cash equivalents, short-term investments, and trading assets, up $80 million &#32;from Q2 2016 . Our business continues to generate healthy cash with $5.8 billion &#32;of cash from operations in Q3 2016 . During Q3 2016 , we purchased $2.5 billion &#32;in capital assets, paid $1.2 billion &#32;in dividends, repurchased $457 million &#32;of common stock, and repaid $1.0 billion in commercial paper. As of October 1, 2016 , total debt was $27.6 billion .
